# Final Touch site (home + six work pages)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: home and business owners in Egypt and the Gulf choosing who designs and finishes their space. Job: show that one team designs, builds and finishes. Action: mail or message the studio (no form). Languages: English and Arabic (RTL), one per page. Approved reference: landing preview round 3 (2026-11-18), source in `brand/preview/landing-v2.src.html`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the job itself. A room is finished in front of the visitor, from bare concrete to the lamp going on. It refuses the category default of a photo slider hero over a services card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Oxblood wall (#3E111B / #34101A / #4A1420), near-black walnut night (#170E10), plaster paper (#EFE8DF, #E2D8CC), ink (#1F1517), lamp gold (#E9BE86) used only as light. Noto Serif Display 300 for display, Hanken Grotesk for text, Oooh Baby as the pen signature; Markazi Text, Readex Pro and Aref Ruqaa in Arabic. The logo's slanted tile is the door and the step tick. Square corners, hairline rules, no cards.

STORY: This is how they leave a room; this is how they find it; every layer in between is theirs, including the workshop. Then the visitor sees the work by field and writes to them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Oxblood wall filling the frame; upper right, the slanted tile cut through it as a glass door about half the viewport high with the lit room behind and FT written on the glass; lower left, "We finish what we design." at up to 96px, one line of text under it and a scroll cue. Header floats over the wall. Scrolling is the primary action.

FORM: Scroll film of one 3D room (direction 2 of 4, The Finished Room), then process walk, 3D gallery wall, workshop, contact. seed:18af5564e1f66ad7

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

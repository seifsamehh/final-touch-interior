How a project runs

1

We plan it

We visit the space, measure it and draw how it will be laid out.

  • Site visit and survey
  • Layout options
  • Working drawings
2

We show it

Every room is rendered in 3D before anything is ordered.

  • 3D views of every space
  • Materials and colours
  • Revisions until you approve
3

We make it

The joinery and the furniture are built in our own workshop.

  • Doors and panelling
  • Kitchens and dressing rooms
  • Sofas, beds and tables
4

We finish it

Our crews finish on site, layer by layer, until handover.

  • Plaster, floors and paint
  • Installation
  • Handover
